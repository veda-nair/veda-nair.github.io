Pro segmentation for safe rollouts

Adobe Photoshop, 2025–2026

The launch
Photoshop added an AI-assisted editor: a new editing space where people describe edits in plain language, alongside the traditional Pro Editor.
The problem

What I built
A model that picks out millions of Creative Pros from Photoshop's user base. The AI-assisted editor's launch used it to bring the new experience to the right people while protecting professionals' existing workflows.

The new editor shipped without regressions to the workflows professionals depend on.

Finding where the AI Assistant fails

Adobe Photoshop, 2025–present

The problem
Adobe opened the Photoshop AI Assistant as a beta on the web and mobile, letting people edit by describing changes in plain language. With hundreds of thousands of beta users, frustrating responses were scattered across thousands of conversations, with no way to see the patterns.
What I built
A failure-pattern analysis system that runs evaluations over LangSmith traces, groups failures by cause, and points engineers to the fixes with the biggest payoff.

Faster, targeted fixes for the failures that frustrated beta users most.

Python, LangSmith, LLM evaluation, SQL

Skill segmentation for guided learning

Adobe Photoshop, 2024 internship

The problem
Every Photoshop user saw the same tutorials and GenAI prompts, whether they'd opened the app once or used it daily for a decade.
What I built
A skill segmentation model, built from scratch, that sorts millions of Photoshop users by proficiency and routes each to tutorial paths and GenAI experiences that match. It powers ATLAS, Photoshop's guided tutorial system.

Beginners succeeded more often in their first week, and the labels are now used across Adobe for personalization and experiment gating.

Python, PySpark, Databricks, scikit-learn
